Our Philosophy

Landscapes that are as visually appealing
as they are ecologically beneficial.

The Well-Kept Habitat promotes the design and care for gardens that work on two levels at once — a refined, intentional landscape for the people who live with it, and a functioning ecosystem for the pollinators, birds, and soil life that depend on it. Inspired by the charm of various native perrineals, we believe beauty and ecology are not a trade-off. They are the same goal.

A standard worth holding to.
